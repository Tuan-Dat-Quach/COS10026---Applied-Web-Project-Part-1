Environmental Project Officer

Reference Number: GS102A

The Environmental Project Officer supports the planning, coordination and delivery of environmental conservation projects including tree planting, habitat restoration and community sustainability programs.

Salary $75,000 - $85,000 per year
Employment Type Full-time
Reporting Line Environmental Programs Manager

Key Responsibilities

  1. Coordinate environmental conservation projects.
  2. Organise tree planting and habitat restoration activities.
  3. Work with volunteers and community organisations.
  4. Monitor project progress and prepare reports.
  5. Assist with environmental education programs.

Essential Requirements

  • Relevant qualification or experience in environmental conservation.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Good organisational and time-management skills.
  • Ability to work outdoors when required.
  • Commitment to environmental sustainability.

Preferable Requirements

  • Project management experience.
  • Experience working with volunteers.
  • Knowledge of local environmental issues.
  • Experience working with community organisations.

Community Engagement Coordinator

Reference Number: GS204B

The Community Engagement Coordinator develops relationships with volunteers, schools, local councils and community groups to promote Greener Society's environmental programs.

Salary $65,000 - $75,000 per year
Employment Type Full-time
Reporting Line Community Programs Manager

Key Responsibilities

  1. Recruit and coordinate environmental volunteers.
  2. Organise community conservation events.
  3. Develop relationships with schools and community groups.
  4. Promote Greener Society programs and campaigns.
  5. Support community communication activities.

Essential Requirements

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong interpersonal skills.
  • Good event organisation skills.
  • Ability to work effectively with diverse communities.
  • Interest in environmental sustainability.

Preferable Requirements

  • Previous community engagement experience.
  • Social media or digital communication experience.
  • Event coordination experience.
  • Experience managing volunteers.

Environmental Education Officer

Reference Number: GS305C

The Environmental Education Officer delivers educational activities and workshops that help schools and communities understand sustainability, conservation and protection of local ecosystems.

Salary $70,000 - $80,000 per year
Employment Type Full-time
Reporting Line Environmental Education Manager

Key Responsibilities

  1. Develop and deliver environmental education workshops.
  2. Conduct presentations for schools and community groups.
  3. Prepare educational resources and activities.
  4. Support tree planting and conservation events.
  5. Evaluate the effectiveness of educational programs.

Essential Requirements

  • Relevant education, environmental or community qualification.
  • Excellent presentation and communication skills.
  • Ability to work with students and community members.
  • Strong teamwork skills.
  • Commitment to environmental education.

Preferable Requirements

  • Teaching or training experience.
  • Environmental science knowledge.
  • Experience developing educational resources.
  • Public speaking experience.
